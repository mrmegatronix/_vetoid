VetoID Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 27 November 2026 • Jurisdiction: New Zealand (Privacy Act 2020)

Zero-PII Guarantee: VetoID performs all 18+ age verification strictly on-device in volatile memory (RAM). VetoID does not upload, transmit, sell, or store patron ID photos, names, addresses, dates of birth, or licence/passport numbers.

1. Camera & On-Device OCR Processing

VetoID requests camera access solely to read dates of birth, expiry dates, document types, and accredited NZ Digital ID QR codes on-device to verify patron age under the New Zealand Sale and Supply of Alcohol Act 2012. Captured frames are processed locally in RAM and discarded immediately after displaying the verification verdict.

2. No Biometrics or Facial Recognition

VetoID does not perform facial recognition, biometric template extraction, or automated face matching. Visual likeness verification is performed exclusively by trained human venue staff.

3. Anonymous Shift Logs & End-of-Day Backups

To support Host Responsibility and District Licensing Committee (DLC) compliance, VetoID stores only anonymous aggregate counters (total scans, Pass, Under-18, Expired, Invalid Doc, Headcount) and anonymous scan timestamps paired with staff role. These records contain zero patron personal information.

4. Local Venue Trespass Register

Where a licensed venue elects to record a Trespass Notice issued under the New Zealand Trespass Act 1980, entries are stored exclusively on the venue's local device behind a PIN-protected staff gate and remain under the direct control of the venue's Duty Manager and Privacy Officer.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Analytics

VetoID has no cloud backend, no third-party advertising SDKs, and no tracking analytics. Zero patron data leaves the device.

6. Contact

For privacy inquiries or support regarding VetoID, contact the repository maintainer via github.com/mrmegatronix/_vetoid.
