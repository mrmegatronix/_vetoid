Quick Troubleshooting

  • Camera won't scan in dim lighting: Tap TORCH and tilt the glossy ID card slightly to eliminate laminate glare.
  • Worn or cracked ID card: Tap MANUAL DOB to use the smart date keypad (IPP 4 manual check).
  • Staff Shift & Settings PIN: Tap SHIFT and enter your venue PIN configured in .env.
  • Printing Staff Practice Cards: Open TEST / DRILL or SHIFT and tap PRINT TEST IDs.
